SplitMate AI

SplitMate AI is a bill-splitting app designed to make shared expenses simple. Users can add people and items, assign quantities, calculate individual totals, combine payments, add tips, save bills and share clear summaries.

  • Intelligent receipt scanning
  • Item and quantity assignment
  • Combined payments
  • Tip calculations
  • Multiple currencies
  • Saved bill history
  • Shareable summaries
  • Spin-the-wheel payer selection

About

An independent studio with a product-first mindset.

Saad Digital is an independent app studio founded by Christopher Saad, a South African web developer and product builder focused on creating useful digital tools for everyday life.

The studio combines practical problem-solving, thoughtful interface design and modern development tools to turn real-world ideas into accessible mobile experiences.
