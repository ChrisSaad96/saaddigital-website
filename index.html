Mivaro

Never miss what matters.

Everything important, all in one place.

Mivaro is an organisation and reminder app that helps you stay ahead of important dates, deadlines, documents and recurring responsibilities—across life, work and study.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play
  • Reminders, categories and notifications
  • Repeating and plan-based reminder options
  • Files, Photos and Camera document import

  • Local-first document storage
  • Account and metadata sync
