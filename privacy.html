Website Privacy Policy

Saad Digital Website Privacy Policy.

This policy explains how information is handled when you visit saaddigital.co.za or contact Saad Digital through this website. It does not describe data handling inside Saad Digital apps.

Last updated: 11 August 2026

About this website

Saad Digital operates this public website to provide information about the studio and its apps, publish legal and support information, and provide contact details. The website does not require an account or login.

Technical request information

When you request a page, the hosting service and the systems used to deliver the website may process standard technical information such as an IP address, browser and device information, the page requested, request time, referring page, and basic error or security logs. This information is used as needed to deliver, secure, and maintain the website.

Hosting

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information to provide the hosting service, maintain reliability, prevent abuse, and secure its platform. GitHub handles that information under its own terms and privacy documentation.

Information you send to us

If you contact Saad Digital by email, we receive the email address you use, your message, and any screenshots or attachments you choose to provide. We use this information to understand, manage, and respond to your enquiry and, where reasonably necessary, to address legal or security matters.

Please avoid sending passwords, payment card details, identity documents, or other sensitive information unless it is genuinely necessary for your request.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The Saad Digital website currently does not add analytics tools, advertising trackers, marketing profiling tools, or a cookie consent system. The hosting provider may still process standard request information as described above. If the website's practices change, this policy will be updated.

External links

The website links to app stores and other third-party websites. When you follow an external link, the destination service applies its own privacy practices. Saad Digital does not control those third-party services.

Saad Digital apps

Each Saad Digital app has its own Privacy Policy describing how that app handles information, device permissions, and third-party services.

  • Mivaro Privacy Policy
  • SplitMate AI Privacy Policy

Security and retention

Saad Digital takes reasonable care when handling information received through the website. No website or email system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Email correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide support, maintain business records, or meet legal and security requirements, and may then be deleted when no longer needed.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website or its information-handling practices change. The date at the top of this page shows the latest material update.

Contact

For questions about this website policy, email saaddigitalapps@gmail.com.
