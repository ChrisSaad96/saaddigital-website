Privacy Policy

General website privacy policy.

This page explains how visitors may interact with the Saad Digital website and how app-specific privacy practices may be described separately.

Important notice

This website policy is general information and should be reviewed before final commercial publication. It is not legal advice.

Information this website may receive

Like most websites, this site may receive standard technical information through hosting and browser requests. This can include IP address, browser type, device information, pages requested, referring pages, request times and basic error or security logs.

Hosting

The Saad Digital website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information needed to deliver the site, maintain service reliability and protect the platform.

Email communications

If you contact Saad Digital by email, your email address, message content and any attachments are processed so the request can be read, managed and answered. Avoid sending sensitive information unless it is necessary for your request.

Apps and third-party services

Saad Digital apps may have app-specific privacy practices described separately in the relevant app, store listing or privacy disclosure. Depending on the app and feature, third-party services may be used, including analytics, advertising, subscriptions, app-store services, receipt recognition, OCR or similar providers.

Users should refer to the relevant app's disclosures for details about what applies to that app.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact saaddigital@gmail.com.
