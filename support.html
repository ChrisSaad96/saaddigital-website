Before you email

  • Check that you are using the latest app version available to you.
  • Restart the app and try the action again.
  • Describe steps that reproduce the problem, if possible.
  • Include a screenshot or screen recording only when useful.

Do not send passwords, payment card details, identity documents, or other sensitive information unless genuinely necessary for support.

Mivaro Support

Organise reminders and documents locally.

Help for Mivaro's current local-first reminders, files, and preferences.

Reminders and notifications

  • Notifications require device permission.
  • Check Mivaro's Notification Settings and the app's notification permission in device settings.
  • Confirm the due date, reminder date, recurrence, time zone, and device time are correct.
  • Delivery can depend on operating-system behaviour, battery settings, and device state.

For truly critical deadlines, keep an independent record as well as a Mivaro reminder.

Documents

  • Mivaro currently saves added images and PDFs in app-managed local storage on your device.
  • Image documents can be viewed inside Mivaro.
  • PDF viewing or opening can depend on the current in-app viewer, device, and installed system apps.
  • If a local file is unavailable, remove or replace the document using the available app controls.

Local data

Mivaro is currently local-first and does not offer Saad Digital account-based cloud backup or sync. Uninstalling the app, clearing its storage, resetting or losing the device, or losing access to a file can remove local data. Keep separate copies of important documents.

SplitMate AI Support

Set up, scan, review, and share a bill.

Help for calculations, receipt recognition, sharing, ads, and local bill history.

Bill setup and calculations

  • Confirm each person, item, quantity, price, tip, assignment, currency, and combined payment.
  • Correct duplicate or missing items before finalising.
  • Review individual totals and the full bill summary before relying on or sharing it.

SplitMate AI calculates bill information but does not transfer money or process payments.

Receipt scanning and OCR

  • Use clear, even lighting and avoid glare or heavy shadows.
  • Keep the receipt flat and fully visible.
  • Retake the image if text is blurry, folded, faded, or cut off.
  • Verify every extracted item, quantity, price, tax, and total.

Receipt recognition can make mistakes and requires user review.

Camera and gallery access

If receipt capture or selection is unavailable, check SplitMate AI's camera and photo permissions in device settings. Permission is requested for the receipt action you choose. Disabling it can make the related option unavailable.

Sharing

Sharing uses compatible apps installed on your device. Check that the intended sharing app is available and up to date. Review the summary and recipient before sending; the chosen third-party service applies its own terms and privacy practices.

Ads and rewarded ads

Where ads are enabled, their availability can depend on your connection, region, privacy choices, and available ad inventory. Rewarded ads are optional and user-initiated where offered. If a stated reward is not applied, keep the app open briefly, check your connection, and try again before contacting support.

Local bill history

Saved bills are stored locally on the device. Removing the app, clearing app storage, resetting or losing the device, or deleting a saved bill may permanently remove that history.
