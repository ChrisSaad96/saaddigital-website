Mivaro · by Saad Digital

Delete your Mivaro account.

You can start deletion inside Mivaro. If you no longer have access to the app, you can send a deletion request to Saad Digital from this page.

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Recommended

Delete your account in Mivaro

If you can sign in to Mivaro, use the in-app deletion process:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Select Account.
  3. Select Delete account and follow the confirmation steps.

The authenticated in-app route is the most direct way to confirm that the request belongs to the account owner.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access Mivaro, email Saad Digital from the address associated with your Mivaro account. State that you want to delete your Mivaro account and include your platform (iOS or Android). We may ask for reasonable information to verify account ownership before acting on the request.

Do not send your password, authentication codes, payment-card details or copies of identity documents.

Request account deletion

Request address: saaddigitalapps@gmail.com

What deletion covers

Deletion is intended to remove Mivaro-controlled information associated with your account, including:

  • Mivaro profile and account information
  • Reminders and account-owned Mivaro information
  • Document records and synced Mivaro metadata
  • Mivaro-managed local account data and files when removed through the app's deletion process

What deletion does not remove

Deleting Mivaro does not delete:

  • Your Google account or Apple Account
  • Original documents stored separately in iCloud Drive, Files, Photos or another source
  • Apple App Store or Google Play purchase history
  • Records that must be retained for a limited period for legal, transaction, security, fraud-prevention or support purposes

Manage your subscription separately

Deleting your Mivaro account or uninstalling the app does not necessarily cancel an active subscription. To prevent future renewal, manage or cancel Premium through the same store account used to subscribe.

  • Manage an Apple subscription
  • Manage a Google Play subscription

Need help?

For questions about deletion, contact saaddigitalapps@gmail.com. You can also review the Mivaro Privacy Policy, Mivaro Terms of Use and Mivaro support information.
