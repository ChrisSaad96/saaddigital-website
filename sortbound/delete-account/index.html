Request permanent deletion of your Sortbound: Color Sort Puzzle account and associated personal data. You can email Saad Digital without reinstalling the game, or use the in-game process where account services are enabled.

Request account deletion In-game instructions

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Before you request deletion

Completed account deletion may be irreversible. You may lose access to account-linked progress, coins, boosters, settings and purchase benefits. Export anything you want to keep before starting, where the game provides that option.

Local guest play does not itself create an online account. Some versions of Sortbound have account services disabled. If you have only played locally, an online account may not exist to delete; contact us if you are unsure or need help with a privacy request.

Delete an account in Sortbound

These steps apply in a version with account services enabled:

  1. Open Sortbound. If necessary, open Settings → Profile and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings → Privacy & support → Manage my data.
  3. Read Delete your online account. Use Export my game data first if you want a separate copy and that option is available.
  4. Type DELETE in the confirmation field, then select Delete account.
  5. Complete any required Apple or Google reauthentication for the same account. If both providers are linked, both may need verification. An Apple-linked account may require an Apple device for this step.
  6. Wait for a confirmed result. A pending request, network error or interrupted sign-in is not confirmation that deletion is complete.

If the account controls are unavailable, you cannot sign in, or you no longer have the game, use the email request option below. You do not need to reinstall Sortbound to contact us.

Request deletion without the game

Email saaddigitalapps@gmail.com with the subject Sortbound Account Deletion Request. State that you want your Sortbound account and associated personal data deleted.

Identify the account email address, including an Apple relay address if that is what you used, and whether you signed in with Apple or Google. Send from the associated address where possible. If you cannot access it, explain that you need another way to verify ownership.

An email address alone is not proof of ownership. We may require additional verification through the supported account process before any destructive action. Sending an email starts a request; it does not automatically delete an account. We will explain any further steps or service limitations and confirm completion only after the verified backend deletion process, including required provider work, has completed.

Never send passwords, access tokens or other authentication tokens, verification codes, private deletion receipts, recovery codes, payment-card details or identity documents. Complete any required sign-in verification through the supported provider flow, not by emailing credentials. Only provide information needed to identify and verify the request.

Account data covered by deletion

Where an online account exists, the verified deletion process is designed to remove:

  • The Sortbound authentication account, account-linked profile information and access to it.
  • Account-owned cloud game progress, recovery archives, checkpoints and associated saved preferences.
  • The account wallet, coin and booster balances, and account-linked gameplay, reward and device-registration records.
  • Account-linked legal-choice records and applicable billing/customer profiles, subject to required provider processing and the limited retained records described below.

When the in-game process confirms completion, it also clears the account's sign-in state and saved profile on that device and cancels its local reminders. A website or email request cannot remotely erase independent files or every copy on another device. Exported files and separate device or platform backups may remain; remove copies you no longer need using their own controls.

Records that may be retained

Limited transaction/refund evidence, duplicate-purchase and reward-prevention markers, deletion receipts, security records and support correspondence may need to remain for legitimate legal, fraud-prevention, dispute or recovery purposes. Removing the game-account association does not necessarily make every retained identifier anonymous.

Retention must be limited to what is needed for the relevant purpose or required by law. No fixed retention period or immediate erasure of every record is promised here. Contact us for the handling applicable to your request, including any lawful exception, unresolved provider work or records that must remain.

Apple, Google and other providers may retain their own store, transaction, security or legally required records under their separate policies. Deleting a Sortbound account does not delete your Apple Account or Google account. Any separate support-message deletion request should be identified in your email.

Coins, boosters and purchases

Account-linked coins, boosters, saved progress and applicable purchase benefits may no longer be accessible after deletion. They are not transferred to a new account, and deleting an account does not automatically refund purchases or restore spent consumables.

Sortbound's current purchase design uses consumables rather than subscriptions. Account deletion is not a store-subscription cancellation or a refund request. Manage any applicable store subscription and refund request separately through Apple App Store or Google Play.

Nothing on this page removes mandatory consumer or privacy rights. Contact support about a pending purchase or refund before clearing device storage.

If a request is interrupted or pending

Keep the game and its stored recovery information intact while a deletion remains unconfirmed. In the signed-out Manage my data area, use Check pending account deletion where available. You may need to sign in again with the same provider to complete verification.

Do not clear storage or repeatedly create another account to resolve a pending request. Contact support if a check fails, a provider cannot be verified or the service is unavailable. An error, unknown status or lost response is not treated as completed deletion.

Requests are subject to ownership verification and applicable legal requirements. Where extra steps, time or lawful retention are needed, we will explain the position and next steps. This page does not provide an automated deletion or status-check service.

Signing out and uninstalling are different

Signing out locks the local profile; it does not delete the online account. Uninstalling or clearing local data may remove guest progress or recovery information, but does not itself request deletion of an online account, store records or support emails.
